\section{Other Programs of Note}
There are two other pieces of software that should also be mentioned.
Pulled\_Pork and Oinkmaster are both programs that can download rules and update
your Snort sensor with them, and even do some of the more advanced features you
will find in Snowman and BHTB. Pulled\_Pork even has support for the Shared
Object rules, something this project has not taken into account due to
complexity. 

However, both of these programs suffer from the fact that they are
command line oriented programs. They are both very good at what they do, but do not have the userability and
efficiency that you get from a GUI with buttons. In a limited environment with
one or very few Snort sensors, they are probably a better choice for rule
management than something like BHTB or Snowman. But once the environment grows
into a more professional one, with tens or even hundreds of sensors, they would
start to slow the user down.
